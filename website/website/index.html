About Us:

Vision:

Absolute Enlightenment through Knowledge.

This is embedded in our logo which forms the core-value of the institution. This institution is committed to provide high quality, affordable education to all section of society. Being the critical component of human development, the college aims to equip our student with knowledge and skill that allow them to make grater contribution to society. Our motto is to prepare our students to face new challenges of the inexorable mark of technology, solving growing demands and aspiration of the society in unlocking and harnessing new knowledge and innovation ideas, building cultural understanding and modelling environment that promote dialogue and debate. We believe that "We cannot always build future for youth but we can build youth for future."

Mission:

1. To promote scientific temper among the student to adapt to technological advancement and innovation ideas.
2. To inculcate ethical, human, and cultural values imbued with a sense of participation facilitating holistic growth.
3. To provide education accessible to all sections of society.
4. To prepare the future generation of a skilled professionals with essential scruples to compete in a diverse global environment.
5. To encourage critical thinking and analytical skills among the students to instill sense of responsibility actuated by indomitable courage to scale new height of excellence.
6. To inculcate "Gender Equality" and "Respect for Individual Rights" in a multicultural society as the sacrosanct principle.

RULES & REGULATIONS:

Disciplinary Code For Students.

1. Every student must carry his/her College Identity Card to College. This must be prominently displayed on the dress.
2. As per B.R.A. Bihar University rules, 75% attendance of all the classes held in the respective departments is binding on each student. Students who report sick or miss classes for any serious cause will have to report to the Heads of the Departments who will then decide in which academic manner the attendance shortfall is to be compensated. In all other cases of shortage of class attendance, the concerned students will not be allowed to sit for the examination of that particular year..
3. Ragging is a criminal offense. Any student found guilty of ragging will face stringent legal and/or punitive action as per UGC and B.R.A. Bihar University guidelines.
4. Gender harassment is strictly prohibited. Any act of gender harassment, as identified in the Vishakha Guidelines set by the Supreme Court of India, will be dealt with accordingly.
5. Admitted students are expected to behave responsibly and courteously on campus and beyond.
6. Each student of vocational courses must wear the prescribed uniform as per their department rules.
7. The use of Mobile phones by students is strictly prohibited in class. If caught with an activated mobile phone in class, the phone will be seized and the concerned student will be forwarded to the Principal/ HOD.
8. M. P. Sinha Science College, Muzaffarpur has been declared a “Tobacco Free Zone”. Violators will be punished accordingly by the Principal/HOD.
9. No meetings are to be held inside the college campus without the Principal’s consent.
